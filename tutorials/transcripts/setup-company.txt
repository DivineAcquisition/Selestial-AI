Let's set up your company, so everything Selestial prepares fits the way you actually work.

Start with your company name and time zone. Schedules, availability and deadlines all run on this clock.

Choose how to begin. Selestial can read your website or your records, but entering the details yourself is a few short questions.

Add where you're based and the areas you serve, in your own words. Then choose who you clean for. These shape the services and booking pages Selestial drafts for you. Nothing is priced or published yet.

Next, how your cleaners work with you, and roughly how many. Employees and contractors get different onboarding and pay rules later on.

Then pick what you'd like help with first. You can add the others any time, and setup saves as you go, so you can finish the rest later.

Two settings change real bookings, so they live in Settings, under Company profile. Add the ZIP codes you serve, and new bookings are only accepted inside them. Then set your booking hours. Each cleaner's own availability still applies.

That's your company set up. Next, build your services and pricing, so customers and your agents work from real prices.
